THE PROBLEM

Far from Earth, help takes time a crew may not have.

3–22 minOne-way signal delay between Earth and Mars.
OfflineNo internet. It all runs on one small computer on board.
Plain wordsCrew describe symptoms the way people really talk. VITALS asks one follow-up at a time.

HOW IT WORKS

An AI that has to show its sources.

01Listen. Findings are read from the complaint and shown back, so a misreading can be fixed.
02Narrow. 14 spaceflight conditions and 72 findings, each tied to NASA sources.
03Reason. A local model ranks only those conditions.
04Double-check. A rule engine always votes on how serious it is.
05Answer. A level, what to do now, and a source link for every condition.
