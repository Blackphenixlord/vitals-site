01 · The problem

Help is 22 minutes away

On Mars, a radio signal takes about 3 to 22 minutes to reach Earth, one way. Asking a flight surgeon a question and hearing back can take the better part of an hour, and when the Sun sits between the planets, contact can drop out for about two weeks.

A crew may not include a doctor. When someone gets sick, they have to decide on their own: is this routine, or is it an emergency?

02 · Our goal

Help a crew member with no medical training assess symptoms and know how urgently to act, on board, with no link to Earth.

A
Fully offline. No internet, no cloud.
B
Grounded. Every answer is tied to a cited medical source.
C
Repeatable. The same symptoms always get the same answer.
D
No fake data. Sensor slots stay empty until real hardware fills them.
