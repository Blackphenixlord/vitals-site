06 · Hardware

Sensors & computer

The AI runs on an NVIDIA Jetson inside Docker, with the model built into the image so it starts with no download. Sensors are planned on a Raspberry Pi.

Heart rate + SpO₂
SELECTED
MAX30102 pulse oximeter: reads the pulse from light through the fingertip
Blood pressure
IN PROGRESS
Salvaged cuff, micro pump and valve, a 0–40 kPa pressure sensor, and a MOSFET drive circuit
Temperature
EVALUATING
Comparing contact and non-contact sensors
Respiration
OPTIONAL
60 GHz mmWave radar, a no-touch option
07 · Interface

Built for the crew

—
Name and password login, plus a red Emergency button that skips it
—
A side rail: chats, profile, vitals, doctor notes and supplies
—
The urgency level comes first, as a word and a color
—
One follow-up question at a time, and a “Wrong?” button to fix a misread
—
44 px touch targets, 4.5:1 contrast, runs fully offline
08 · Testing
