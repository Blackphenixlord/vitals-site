VITALS

An offline AI medical triage assistant for crews too far from Earth to call a doctor

03

How it works

1
Crew describes symptoms
In plain words, typed in: “I’ve had a headache and felt dizzy since this morning.”
2
Extract findings
The description is turned into structured medical findings, and negations like “no fever” are caught.
3
Retrieve conditions
Only the conditions that match are pulled from our cited medical knowledge base.
4
Read vital signs
Heart rate, SpO₂, blood pressure and temperature from on-board sensors (hardware in progress).
5
Local AI reasons
A language model running in Ollama on an NVIDIA Jetson weighs the evidence. No internet needed.
6
Answer + escalation
Likely conditions, an urgency level, the next question to ask, and citations from the knowledge base.
04

Guardrails in the code

01
The AI can only name conditions from the knowledge base. Anything it invents is dropped and reported.
02
It never writes a citation. Sources are looked up from the knowledge base.
03
Temperature 0: the same complaint always gets the same answer, so it can be tested.
04
It tracks what is ALREADY KNOWN and STILL UNKNOWN, so it never re-asks a question.
05
If the AI names a serious condition but forgets to escalate, the code escalates anyway.
05

Four urgency levels

Always shown as a word and a color, never color alone.
EMERGENCY
Act immediately
2 conditions
URGENT
Needs prompt attention
2 conditions
MONITOR
Watch and reassess
5 conditions
ROUTINE
Handle with normal care
